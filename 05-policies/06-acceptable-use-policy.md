## 1. Purpose
To set out how staff and contractors may use Meridiaan's information, devices and systems, so that everyday behaviour does not put security at risk.

## 2. Scope
All information, endpoints, accounts, tooling and network access provided by Meridiaan, whether used on-site or remotely.

## 3. Acceptable use rules
- Use information and systems only for legitimate business purposes and per your access rights.
- Protect credentials; never share them; use MFA as required (A.8.5).
- Handle information according to its classification; do not move client data outside approved systems.
- Keep endpoints secure: apply updates, do not disable security controls, report loss immediately (A.8.1).
- Apply clear desk and clear screen practices in shared and client-visible areas (A.7.7).
- Report suspected security events promptly (A.6.8).
- Do not install unauthorised software on operational systems (A.8.19).

## 4. Monitoring
Use of Meridiaan systems may be monitored and logged for security and compliance, within the limits of law and the privacy policy.

## 5. Compliance
Breaches may lead to withdrawal of access and disciplinary action (A.6.4).
