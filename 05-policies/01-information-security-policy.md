## 1. Purpose
This policy states Meridiaan's commitment to protecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability of its own and its clients' information, and sets the direction for the information security management system (ISMS).

## 2. Scope
The policy applies to all information, systems, staff, sites and suppliers within the ISMS scope defined in `00-scope-context/03-scope-statement.md`.

## 3. Policy statements
- Meridiaan operates a risk-based ISMS aligned to ISO/IEC 27001 and its legal obligations under NIS2 and GDPR, and supports its financial clients' obligations under DORA.
- Information is protected according to its classification, and access is granted on a least-privilege, need-to-know basis.
- Security risks are assessed, treated and reviewed through the ISMS risk process.
- All staff are responsible for security; specific roles are defined in the ISMS roles document.
- Information security incidents are reported, managed and, where required, notified to authorities and clients within statutory and contractual deadlines.
- Meridiaan meets its legal, regulatory and contractual obligations and continually improves the ISMS.

## 4. Responsibilities
- **CEO** — accountable for the ISMS as the management body; approves this policy and resources.
- **CISO** — owns and operates the ISMS and this policy set.
- **All staff and contractors** — comply with this and supporting policies, and report incidents.

## 5. Supporting policies
This policy is supported by the topic-specific policies in `05-policies/`, including access control, incident response, supplier security, business continuity, acceptable use, and cryptography.

## 6. Compliance
Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action under Meridiaan's disciplinary process (A.6.4). Compliance is monitored and reviewed (A.5.36).
