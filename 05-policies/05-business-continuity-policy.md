## 1. Purpose
To ensure Meridiaan can continue to deliver, or promptly recover, its services during and after disruption, and to meet the resilience expectations of its clients, particularly the financial clients.

## 2. Scope
All hosted services, the two hosting sites, HQ operations, and the supporting people and suppliers.

## 3. Policy statements
- Continuity requirements are based on the risk register and on client commitments; recovery objectives (RTO/RPO) are defined per service.
- Meridiaan operates a two-site design with replication so that loss of one site does not cause loss of service (A.8.14, R-01, R-08).
- Backups are taken, protected and tested for restorability (A.8.13, R-05).
- Continuity and disaster-recovery plans are documented, exercised at least annually, and evidenced (A.5.29, A.5.30).
- Climate-related and environmental threats to sites and power are considered in continuity planning (R-16).

## 4. Testing and improvement
Recovery and continuity are tested on a defined schedule and after significant change; results are reviewed and drive improvement. Evidence of tests is retained for audit and client assurance.

## 5. Compliance
Continuity responsibilities are assigned and monitored; failures to test or maintain plans are treated as gaps and remediated (A.5.36).
