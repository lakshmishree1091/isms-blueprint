## 1. Purpose
To manage the information security risks that arise from Meridiaan's suppliers and its ICT supply chain, and to meet the third-party requirements that flow down from its financial clients under DORA.

## 2. Scope
All suppliers whose products or services affect the security or availability of Meridiaan's or its clients' information — in particular the colocation facility operators (critical suppliers), connectivity, software and sub-processors.

## 3. Policy statements
- Suppliers are risk-assessed before onboarding and classified by criticality (A.5.19).
- Security requirements are set out in supplier agreements, proportionate to the risk (A.5.20).
- Critical suppliers, including colocation operators, are subject to enhanced due diligence and ongoing monitoring (A.5.22).
- The ICT supply chain is managed so that a supplier compromise cannot readily reach Meridiaan's clients (A.5.21, R-03, R-11).
- Supplier performance and security are reviewed at defined intervals, and on any incident.

## 4. DORA-driven contractual requirements
For services supporting financial clients' critical or important functions, contracts and arrangements provide, as required by those clients:
- Audit, access and inspection rights.
- Incident notification and cooperation obligations.
- Sub-outsourcing controls and transparency.
- Defined service levels and exit / termination strategies.
- Support for the client's register of information.

## 5. Compliance
Suppliers failing to meet requirements are remediated, escalated or exited. Compliance is evidenced for client assurance and audit (A.5.22, A.5.36).
